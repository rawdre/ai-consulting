Add-Ons

Optional upgrades for the trays — keep food hot, deliver to the door, or have us fully set up the buffet for you.

Wire Chafers & Sternos

Without this upgrade, your food is provided in sealed aluminum trays. Wire chafers and sternos keep everything hot for your guests.

+5% of order totalor $25 minimum

Delivery

Delivery charges are calculated by distance from Fitchburg, MA — both ways. Friendly hand-off to your door or venue.

$1.50 / mile (both ways)or $25 minimum

Setup Service

We will completely set up your buffet so you don't have to.

+5% of order totalor $25 minimum
