FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

Is Comeketo Trays different from Comeketo Catering? +
Yes — Comeketo Trays is a new line from the same team. The main Comeketo Catering site focuses on Brazilian BBQ & full-service catering. Comeketo Trays is the Italian / Mexican / American tray menu, designed to be quick to order online for any-size event.
Can I just order trays without service? +
Absolutely. Order by the tray, we deliver it hot in sealed aluminum, and you take it from there. Pick add-ons (chafers, delivery, setup) only if you need them.
Can Comeketo also set up the buffet? +
Yes. Choose the "Full Setup Service" add-on (+5% of order, $25 min) and our team will dress the tables, warm the trays and have the buffet ready before your first guest arrives.
Do prices include tax or delivery? +
No — the menu shows tray prices only. 7% Massachusetts meals tax is added at checkout, and delivery is calculated by distance from Fitchburg, MA (both ways). Your final confirmed quote will show all line items clearly.
How far do you deliver? +
We serve all of Massachusetts and parts of New England. Delivery is $1.50/mile both ways from Fitchburg, MA, with a $25 minimum. For events farther out, we'll quote it clearly — no surprises.
How early should I place my order? +
As early as you can — a few days is ideal for small orders, and 1–2 weeks for larger events or full buffet setup. We do our best to fit last-minute orders when the kitchen has capacity. Call (978) 381‑1212 if you're tight on time.
What about dietary restrictions and allergies? +
Note allergies, vegetarian counts, or special requests when you submit your order — we'll do our best to accommodate. The menu also includes vegetarian options like Eggplant Parmesan, Veggie Fajitas and Pasta Primavera.
How do I pay? +
A non-refundable deposit confirms your order; the balance is due before or at delivery. Our team will walk you through payment options when they send the final quote.
