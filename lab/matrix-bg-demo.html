Your business,
running itself.

Scroll. Everything behind this text reacts — the rain accelerates with your scroll speed, the light band tracks your position down the page, and the neural web drifts on parallax.

Layer 1 — WebGL shader field

A single full-screen quad running one fragment shader. Simplex noise at two different scales gives you parallax dust; two diagonal sweeps give you light beams; a band of light tracks your scroll position. Roughly 40 lines of GLSL, zero libraries.

Simplex noise

The Ashima Arts snoise function — the canonical copy-paste one. Threshold it with smoothstep and mush becomes stars.

Light sweeps

Two diagonal bands whose brightness rises with your scroll velocity. Scroll fast, the room lights up.

Scroll horizon

A soft band that rises from the bottom to the top as you move down the page. Cyan at the top, green at the bottom.

Layer 2 — Matrix digital rain

Real katakana glyphs, discrete cell stepping so characters never smear, a bright white leading glyph that gets repainted green the moment it moves on, and trails made with destination-out compositing so the canvas stays transparent.

Two depth bands

Near columns are bright, fast and green. Far columns are dim, slow and cyan. That is the whole parallax.

Scroll boost

Fall speed is multiplied by your scroll velocity. Stop scrolling and it settles back down.

Glitch bursts

On hard scrolls, random columns reset with a flash. Cheap, and it sells the machine.

Layer 3 — Neural plexus

Nodes drifting with random velocities. Any two closer than the link distance get a line, opacity fading with distance. Your cursor is an extra node with a longer reach — move the mouse and the web reaches for it.

Layer 4 — CRT + vignette

Pure CSS, free. A repeating 1px scanline gradient that drifts and flickers, plus a radial vignette that darkens the edges so your copy never fights the background. This is what makes the whole thing read as a screen instead of a wallpaper.

Tune it, then ship it

Set the panel the way you want it, hit COPY CONFIG JSON, and paste that object into the CONFIG block of matrix-bg-dropin.html. That file is the paste-ready version for your live site — no panel, no HUD, no demo copy.
