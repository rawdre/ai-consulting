## Bar And Drink Pricing

The PDF is the source of truth for bar structure and add-ons.

Visible pricing from the document:
- Mocktail dispenser station: `$3.00/pp`
- Caipirinha upgrade: `+$2.00/pp`
- Water service at table (disposable): `$3.00/pp`
- Water station: `$2.00/pp`
- Real glass water glass: `$1.25 each`
- Real wine glass: `$1.25 each`
- Real champagne glass: `$1.50 each`
- Champagne service with disposable flutes: `$3.99/pp`
- Champagne service with real flutes: `$5.95/pp`

Top-shelf note:
- Top shelf varies from `+$4.00/pp` to `+$8.00/pp`

Signature drink note:
- Signature drinks shown in the PDF include Rum Punch, Margarita, Moscow Mule, and Daiquiri.
- Caipirinha counts as 2 signature drinks.

Bar service note:
- All bar services in the PDF include up to `4 hours` of service.
- The PDF also explains the difference between cash bar and open bar.
